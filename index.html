A letter for you

My Message to You

To my favorite person,

Seven months. I keep saying it like it’s a big number, and then I remember that it’s also just seven ordinary months — seven months of good mornings, of “did you eat,” of you farting on your sleep and eating good nyamnyams.

I used to think love was this loud, dramatic thing. You taught me it’s softer than that, and quieter, and that it waits. It’s you sending me something in tiktok. because it reminded you of me. It’s me saving the last bite without thinking, because it’s yours. It’s a hundred tiny decisions a day, and every single one of them choosing you.

Thank you for loving me the way you do gently on the days I don’t deserve it, stubbornly on the days I am pasaway or the days na may kasalanan ako. Not once did you make me feel like I had to earn it. Do you know how rare that is? Do you know what that did to me?

Here’s what I want you to know, and I want you to read it slowly: you are not a chapter in my life. You are the whole book, cover to cover. Everything before you was the title page.

Seven months down, and I’ve stopped counting. I’d rather just be in it with you for all the months I haven’t numbered yet, and the ones after those.

Happy 7th monthsary, my love. Thank you for being mine and for letting me be yours.

Always and annoyingly yours,
Me
